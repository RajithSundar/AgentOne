>
> I architected and implemented **AgentOne**, a production-grade multi-agent operations platform built with **LangGraph**, **FastAPI**, and **Hybrid RAG** (Dense Vectors + Okapi BM25 with Reciprocal Rank Fusion). 
>
> The system orchestrates a cooperative network of 5 specialized agents (*Supervisor/Router*, *Triage & Sentiment Specialist*, *RAG & Self-Reflection Grader*, *Action Dispatcher*, and *Critic/Compliance Reviewer*) with built-in **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) checkpoints** for high-risk mutations (e.g., refunds ≥ $500).
