# Billing, Invoicing, and Refund Policy

## 1. Automated Refund Eligibility Rules
- **Direct Refund Authorization**: Transactions under $500.00 USD requested within 30 days of the billing event are eligible for automated processing by the Operations Agent if no previous dispute exists.
- **Human Review Threshold**: Any refund request equal to or exceeding $500.00 USD, or accounts with active fraud alerts, MUST trigger a Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) review checkpoint and cannot be automatically disbursed.
- **Refund Processing Window**: Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Stripe/Card/ACH) within 3 to 5 business days.

## 2. Cancellation and Proration Policy
- Annual subscriptions canceled mid-term receive prorated platform credits applied to the customer workspace balance.
- Monthly plans can be canceled anytime with access maintained until the end of the current billing cycle.

## 3. Dispute Resolution and Chargeback Handling
If a customer initiates a formal bank dispute (chargeback), the billing agent automatically locks external export access while preserving historical audit logs for 90 days.
